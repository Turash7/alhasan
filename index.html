Boiler regular dosing, run as a managed programme

The right dose depends on your boiler pressure, make-up water, condensate return and blowdown practice, so we start with a test, not a price list. Every visit ends in a signed service report.

  1. SurveyBoiler, feedwater and dosing setup
  2. SampleFrom approved sample points
  3. AnalyseHardness, pH, TDS, alkalinity
  4. DoseCalibrated pump or batch feed
  5. MonitorResiduals at the agreed frequency
  6. ReportResults, consumption, actions
  7. AdjustOnly after documented review

Have a recent water test or a nameplate photo? Send it with your request and our engineer will prepare a dosing plan for your boiler.
